01 / TECHNOLOGY & ENGINEERING PRINCIPLES

Aerospace fluid mechanics
engineered for cooling.

CoolJet challenges the century-old reliance on chemical vapor-compression. By harnessing high-velocity blower induction and aerodynamic de Laval nozzle contours, ambient air is conditioned through gas-dynamic expansion rather than chemical phase transitions.

06 / APPLICATIONS & PROJECT IMPACT

Built for critical sectors.
Targeted global impact.

CoolJet’s compressor-free architecture addresses both operational vulnerabilities in mission-critical equipment and global sustainability mandates requiring rapid phase-down of chemical cooling agents.

APP / 01DATA CENTERS & MISSION CRITICAL

High-Density Server Thermal Regulation

Eliminates pressurized liquid refrigerant loops and toxic chemical piping above sensitive server racks and microelectronics. CoolJet provides direct, non-conductive atmospheric airflow cooling with zero leak liability.

  • ↗Zero liquid leak hazard
  • ↗Modular rack enclosure fit
  • ↗Continuous non-chemical uptime
APP / 02COMMERCIAL & RESIDENTIAL BUILDINGS

Refrigerant-Free Indoor Air Conditioning

Replaces traditional split-system indoor and outdoor units with a streamlined, ductless architecture. Simplifies building architectural design by removing complex refrigerant line sets, brazed copper connections, and hazardous evacuation procedures.

  • ↗No refrigerant lines through walls
  • ↗Lightweight indoor chassis
  • ↗Low architectural footprint
APP / 03AEROSPACE & ELECTRIC MOBILITY

Electric Vehicle & Cabin Climate Management

Weight and safety are paramount in electric aviation, mass transit rail, and passenger vehicles. CoolJet’s single-blower, compressor-less design dramatically cuts mass while eliminating flammable or toxic refrigerant risks during impact.

  • ↗Significant structural weight savings
  • ↗Zero flammability/toxicity risks
  • ↗Direct DC bus motor compatibility
APP / 04TELECOM & INDUSTRIAL ENCLOSURES

Remote Telecom & Switchgear Cabinets

Remote cell towers and utility substations face high service costs when conventional AC units suffer compressor failure or refrigerant depletion. CoolJet offers ultra-high reliability with minimal mechanical servicing.

  • ↗Ideal for remote unmanned sites
  • ↗Low maintenance filter-only care
  • ↗High resistance to mechanical shock
