========================================================================
COOLJET PROTOTYPE VIDEO INTEGRATION
========================================================================

To display your CoolJet video on the website:

OPTION 1 (Recommended for Vercel deployment):
1. Place your video file in this directory (`public/`)
2. Name the file: `cooljet-video.mp4`
   (Path: `public/cooljet-video.mp4`)
3. The video player in the website will automatically detect and play it!

OPTION 2 (Interactive in-browser testing):
- You can also click the "Browse Video File" button directly on the website
  in the "04 / PROTOTYPE DEMONSTRATION" section to immediately test any 
  video from your computer without restarting the server.

Supported Formats:
- MP4 (H.264 / AAC)
- WebM
- QuickTime MOV
